Questions clients ask

What does “Verified Service Partner” mean?

ClickUp runs a partner program for consultants who implement ClickUp for clients. I signed the partner agreement and completed ClickUp’s partner training, and Smart Vision AI is listed as a service partner. It does not make me a ClickUp employee: I work for you, not for ClickUp.

How is buying ClickUp through you priced?

You see the plan and its price before you decide. Implementation work is priced separately in the written scope.

We are on the free plan. Should we upgrade?

Only if a paid feature solves a real problem: more automations, guests, time tracking reports, dashboards or permissions. I tell you which plan fits — including when the free plan is enough.

Can you help a team that already has a paid plan?

Yes. Audit, cleanup, automations and onboarding are useful on any plan.
