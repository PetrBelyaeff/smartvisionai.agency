Case · Travel & tourism

A 24/7 AI sales agent that knows every tour: a regional tour operator

A tour operator with about 30 tours and 100 accommodation options needed every inquiry answered quickly and correctly — including at night and on weekends — and passed to managers ready for a call.

  • amoCRM
  • Suvvy AI agents
  • n8n
  • WhatsApp
  • Telegram
  • Website chat

The situation

Inquiries come from the website and several messengers. Questions are detailed: dates, group size, what is included, where to stay. Answering well requires knowing the whole catalog, and managers were spending their day on first replies instead of selling.

The project was agreed as a fixed-price build in five stages, with a written specification and acceptance criteria.

What I built

  • Knowledge base of tours and accommodation, structured so the agent finds the right option instead of guessing.
  • Two-agent design: a main sales agent leads the conversation, a catalog sub-agent searches tours and accommodation and returns structured options.
  • Qualification and handoff: the agent collects dates, group and preferences, offers a call or an online meeting and passes the lead to a manager in amoCRM.
  • Channels: website and messengers, all landing in one CRM pipeline.
  • Client-owned infrastructure: the automation server belongs to the client, with daily backups.

Why two agents instead of one

One agent that holds the conversation and also searches a large catalog tends to do both badly: long instructions, slow answers, mixed-up options. Splitting the catalog search into a sub-agent keeps the sales agent focused on the client and makes catalog answers consistent and easy to test.

Where it stands

The agent is built in stages against the agreed specification; each stage is accepted by the client before the next one starts. Every change to the agents is recorded in a change log.
