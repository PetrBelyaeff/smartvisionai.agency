A franchise operations platform in ClickUp with an AI assistant: a barbershop chain

A barbershop brand packaging itself as a franchise needed one place for standards and rules that every new franchisee follows — and a way to answer their questions without the founders answering each one.

  • ClickUp
  • ClickUp Docs
  • AI assistant
  • Google Drive

The situation

Franchising only works when a new location can run by the same rules as the original. The founders had the knowledge in their heads, in call recordings and in scattered documents.

New franchisees ask the same questions: how to hire and motivate barbers, what to post on social media, how a shift runs.

What I built

  • Franchise platform in ClickUp: the operating standards as structured ClickUp Docs in the brand’s own workspace.
  • Team rules: compensation and motivation rules for barbers and administrators, written as clear policies instead of ad-hoc fines.
  • SMM guide for franchisees: what to post and how, as a ready document.
  • Franchise presentation for prospective franchisees.
  • AI assistant that answers franchisees from the same knowledge base, updated whenever the standards change.

Where it stands

The platform is being filled section by section with the founders. When a policy changes, the document and the assistant’s knowledge are updated together and checked, so the assistant never contradicts the standard.
