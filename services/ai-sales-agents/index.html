Service · AI agents

AI sales & support agents that answer every lead and know when to call a person

An agent replies in seconds in WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or your website chat, qualifies the lead, books the next step and writes everything to your CRM. When a conversation needs a human, it hands over with full context.

What the agent does

The goal is not a chatbot that talks. The goal is fewer lost leads and more booked calls, with your team in control.

  • Answers 24/7 from an approved knowledge base: services, prices, terms, documents, FAQs.
  • Qualifies the lead with the questions your best manager would ask, and records the answers in CRM fields.
  • Moves the deal: offers a call, books a slot, sends a presentation or a payment link.
  • Follows up when a client goes quiet, with polite reminders on a schedule you control.
  • Hands over to a manager on clear triggers — and tells the client what happens next.
  • Logs everything in the CRM, so a manager picks up the conversation without asking the client to repeat.

How I build it

Most of the work is not the AI itself but the sales logic around it.

  1. Map the conversationWhere leads come from, what they ask, what a qualified lead looks like, where your managers lose time today.
  2. Build the knowledge baseServices, prices, documents and objections collected into a source the agent can rely on. You approve it before launch.
  3. Write the instructions and rulesTone, qualification questions, what the agent must never promise, when it escalates. Tested on real past conversations.
  4. Connect channels and CRMMessengers, website chat and Kommo/amoCRM or Bitrix24, with fields, pipeline stages and manager notifications.
  5. Test, launch, watchA test period with daily review of conversations, then fixes. After launch the agent stays under monitoring.

A good fit if

  • Most of your leads arrive in messengers or website chat.
  • Managers answer the same questions every day.
  • Leads that write at night or on weekends go cold.
  • You have a CRM — or are ready to set one up.
Honest limits. An agent will not fix an unclear offer or a sales process nobody follows. If I see that the bottleneck is elsewhere — pricing, the website, the team’s workflow — I will say so on the first call.

Questions clients ask

Will the agent replace my sales team?

No. The agent takes the repetitive first part of the conversation — answering, qualifying, reminding — and hands warm leads to people. Your managers spend their time on conversations that can close.

What if the agent says something wrong?

The agent answers only from the knowledge base we approve together, and it has explicit rules for what it must not promise: prices outside the list, deadlines, guarantees. Unknown questions go to a person instead of being guessed. Every change to the agent’s instructions is logged, so any answer can be traced back.

Which messengers and CRMs do you support?

WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, website chat and other channels that the agent platform or your CRM can connect. On the CRM side I work with Kommo/amoCRM and Bitrix24 most often; other CRMs are possible through n8n if they have an API.

Which language can the agent speak?

The agent can answer in the language the client writes in. I build and test agents in English, Spanish and Russian.

How long does a launch take?

A first working version for one channel usually takes a few weeks, most of which is building and checking the knowledge base. The exact timeline goes into the written scope before work starts.

What does it cost to run?

There are two parts: my work (a fixed price for the build, then managed services if you need them) and running costs you pay directly — the agent platform, AI model usage and messenger fees. I estimate the running costs in the scope so there are no surprises.
