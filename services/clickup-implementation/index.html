What I set up

A system the team uses without being reminded — and that a manager can read in one glance.

  • Structure: spaces, folders and lists that mirror your services and clients.
  • Statuses and custom fields that match your real workflow, from request to delivery.
  • Automations: assignment, due dates, recurring tasks, notifications.
  • Time tracking and reports by client, project and type of work — ready for billing.
  • Dashboards for the owner: workload, overdue work, what was delivered this week.
  • Client access: guests see their own tasks and updates, nothing else.
  • Docs and knowledge base: standards, instructions and onboarding next to the work.

Questions clients ask

We already use ClickUp, but it’s a mess. Can you fix it?

Yes, and this is the most common starting point. I begin with an audit: what spaces, lists, statuses and fields exist, which of them people actually use, and where work falls through the cracks. Then I propose a target structure and migrate to it without losing history.

Do we need a paid ClickUp plan?

Not always. Many small teams start on the free plan. A paid plan becomes worth it when you need more automations, guests, time tracking reports or dashboards. As a ClickUp Verified Service Partner I can help you choose the plan and arrange it.

Can ClickUp work with our CRM and messengers?

Yes. I connect ClickUp to Kommo/amoCRM, Bitrix24, Telegram, WhatsApp and Google Workspace through n8n or Make — for example, a paid deal in the CRM creates a project in ClickUp, and task updates reach the client’s chat.

How do you get the team to actually use it?

By designing the workspace around how the team already works, not around a template, and by short onboarding sessions with written instructions. A structure that people need to be forced into is a wrong structure.

Can you move us from Trello, Asana, Notion or spreadsheets?

Yes. I plan the migration so that active work moves first and history is preserved where it matters.
