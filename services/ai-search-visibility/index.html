Service · AI search visibility

Does ChatGPT recommend your business — or your competitor?

More buyers now ask AI assistants instead of searching. I check on a schedule whether ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity mention your brand for the questions your buyers ask, which competitors they name instead, and how that changes over time.

Book a call Email me

What you get

A number you can track month to month instead of screenshots and opinions.

  • Baseline: how often AI assistants mention you today across your key questions.
  • Competitor map: which companies the assistants recommend instead of you, and for which questions.
  • Link check: whether your website is cited as a source.
  • History in a Google Sheet: every run, every question, every assistant.
  • A short summary in Telegram or email after each run: what changed and where.

How it works

  1. QuestionsTogether we list the questions real buyers ask an assistant about your niche — usually 30 to 100, in the languages of your market. Questions don’t include your brand name.
  2. Brand dictionaryAll the ways your brand appears: names, spellings, domains. Without it, part of the mentions gets lost.
  3. Scheduled runsAn n8n workflow sends every question to ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity with web search on, several times per run, weekly or monthly.
  4. Answer analysisFor each answer: is the brand mentioned, is the site linked, at what position, which competitors are named.
  5. ReportShare of answers that mention you, compared with the baseline and the previous run.

Useful if

  • You pay for SEO or GEO content and want an independent check of the result.
  • Your buyers research complex decisions — services, travel, education, B2B.
  • You want to know which competitors AI assistants favor, and why.
How it’s priced. A one-time setup (questions, dictionary, workflow, first baseline) plus a monthly fee for scheduled runs and summaries. AI usage costs depend on the number of questions and repeats; I estimate them in the scope before we start.

Questions clients ask

Is this the same as GEO or AEO?

It is the measurement part of it. GEO (generative engine optimization) is the work of getting AI assistants to mention you: content, structure, presence in sources they read. This service tells you whether that work is paying off — independently from whoever does the content.

Do the answers match what my clients see in ChatGPT?

Close, but not identical. I query the models through their APIs with web search on, without a user’s history, location or personalization. That makes the measurement repeatable, which matters more for tracking change than matching one person’s screen.

AI answers change every time. Is the number meaningful?

A single run is a snapshot. That is why each question is asked several times and the result is reported as a share of answers that mention you. The trend over weeks is what you act on.

Do you check Google’s AI Overviews?

Not by default. Google’s AI answers can’t be queried the same way and need a paid search-data service. If they matter for your market, I add them as a separate, priced option.

Can you also improve the results?

The service is built as an honest scoreboard. I can advise on what to change on your site — structure, schema markup, an llms.txt file, answers to the questions buyers ask — and implement the technical part. Content production at scale is better done by a content team.
