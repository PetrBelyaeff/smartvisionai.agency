Questions clients ask

Why n8n and not just the CRM’s built-in automation?

Built-in automation is fine inside one system. When data has to move between a CRM, a store, messengers and ClickUp, n8n gives one place where the whole flow is visible, logged and easy to change. I use Make where a client already has it.

Where does n8n run?

On a server that belongs to you, or on n8n’s cloud. For client projects I prefer a separate server per client: your automations, data and access stay yours, and one client’s problem never touches another’s.

What happens when an integration breaks?

Flows are built with error notifications, so a failure is visible the same day instead of a week later. Under managed services I monitor and fix them; without it, you get documentation good enough for another developer to pick up.

Can you work with our existing setup?

Yes. I usually start by reading what already exists — webhooks, scenarios, pipelines — and keep what works. Nothing is deleted without a backup and your explicit approval.
