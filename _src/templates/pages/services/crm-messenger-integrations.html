Typical flows I build

Each one replaces a manual routine that someone in your team does every day — or forgets to do.

Lead routing

Leads from ads, website forms, TikTok and messengers land in the right CRM pipeline and reach the right manager, with duplicates and spam filtered out.

Order follow-ups

Unpaid orders, abandoned carts and missed payments trigger a WhatsApp message and a CRM task on a schedule.

CRM ↔ ClickUp

A closed deal creates a project in ClickUp; delivery status flows back to the CRM card.

Team notifications

Telegram alerts for new leads, overdue tasks and failures — only the ones people act on.

Client updates

Comments on client tasks reach the client’s chat, so updates don’t depend on someone remembering to send them.

Reporting

Data from CRM, ClickUp and spreadsheets collected into regular reports for the owner.

How I work with integrations

  • Read before changing: existing webhooks, scenarios and pipelines are inventoried first.
  • Backups before every change to a live flow, and a written record of what changed.
  • Secrets stay in a password manager, never in chats, task comments or documents.
  • Error alerts on every production flow.
  • Documentation you can hand to another developer.
