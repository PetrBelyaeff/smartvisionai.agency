What you get

A number you can track month to month instead of screenshots and opinions.

  • Baseline: how often AI assistants mention you today across your key questions.
  • Competitor map: which companies the assistants recommend instead of you, and for which questions.
  • Link check: whether your website is cited as a source.
  • History in a Google Sheet: every run, every question, every assistant.
  • A short summary in Telegram or email after each run: what changed and where.

How it works

  1. QuestionsTogether we list the questions real buyers ask an assistant about your niche — usually 30 to 100, in the languages of your market. Questions don’t include your brand name.
  2. Brand dictionaryAll the ways your brand appears: names, spellings, domains. Without it, part of the mentions gets lost.
  3. Scheduled runsAn n8n workflow sends every question to ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity with web search on, several times per run, weekly or monthly.
  4. Answer analysisFor each answer: is the brand mentioned, is the site linked, at what position, which competitors are named.
  5. ReportShare of answers that mention you, compared with the baseline and the previous run.

Useful if

  • You pay for SEO or GEO content and want an independent check of the result.
  • Your buyers research complex decisions — services, travel, education, B2B.
  • You want to know which competitors AI assistants favor, and why.
How it’s priced. A one-time setup (questions, dictionary, workflow, first baseline) plus a monthly fee for scheduled runs and summaries. AI usage costs depend on the number of questions and repeats; I estimate them in the scope before we start.
