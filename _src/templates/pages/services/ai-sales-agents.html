What the agent does

The goal is not a chatbot that talks. The goal is fewer lost leads and more booked calls, with your team in control.

  • Answers 24/7 from an approved knowledge base: services, prices, terms, documents, FAQs.
  • Qualifies the lead with the questions your best manager would ask, and records the answers in CRM fields.
  • Moves the deal: offers a call, books a slot, sends a presentation or a payment link.
  • Follows up when a client goes quiet, with polite reminders on a schedule you control.
  • Hands over to a manager on clear triggers — and tells the client what happens next.
  • Logs everything in the CRM, so a manager picks up the conversation without asking the client to repeat.

How I build it

Most of the work is not the AI itself but the sales logic around it.

  1. Map the conversationWhere leads come from, what they ask, what a qualified lead looks like, where your managers lose time today.
  2. Build the knowledge baseServices, prices, documents and objections collected into a source the agent can rely on. You approve it before launch.
  3. Write the instructions and rulesTone, qualification questions, what the agent must never promise, when it escalates. Tested on real past conversations.
  4. Connect channels and CRMMessengers, website chat and Kommo/amoCRM or Bitrix24, with fields, pipeline stages and manager notifications.
  5. Test, launch, watchA test period with daily review of conversations, then fixes. After launch the agent stays under monitoring.

A good fit if

  • Most of your leads arrive in messengers or website chat.
  • Managers answer the same questions every day.
  • Leads that write at night or on weekends go cold.
  • You have a CRM — or are ready to set one up.
Honest limits. An agent will not fix an unclear offer or a sales process nobody follows. If I see that the bottleneck is elsewhere — pricing, the website, the team’s workflow — I will say so on the first call.
